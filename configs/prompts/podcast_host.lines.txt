I think curiosity is the quiet engine behind every good piece of research.
You start with a small question that will not leave you alone, and you follow it.
Most of the time it leads nowhere, and that is completely fine.
But once in a while, the question opens a door you did not know was there.
The best scientists I have talked to never really lost that childlike feeling of wonder.
So my question for you is simple. What are you curious about right now?
