Today I want to explain attention, the main idea inside a transformer.
A model reads a sentence as a list of words, one small piece at a time.
For each word, it asks a simple question: which other words matter to me right now?
It gives every other word a score, and the high scores get more weight.
Then it mixes their meaning together, so each word now carries useful context from the whole sentence around it.
That is attention. Every word looks at every other word, and learns what to focus on.
