onegpu-longvideo

Thirty-second one-shot clips with native speech and sound from a joint audio-video diffusion transformer, rendered on one 24 GB consumer GPU, upscaled to 1080p with a streaming SPAN x2 model. All people shown are invented. Code and full notes: github.com/modimorph/onegpu-longvideo.
